# Where the light settles

*Field journal · The Lantern Trail*

![An illustrated mountain lake at dusk](../Attachments/lantern-lake.png)

The path begins at the water's edge. A narrow ribbon of pale stone traces the shoreline, disappearing behind the pines before returning to the open hillside.

## Along the shore

We stopped where the lake held the last light of the afternoon. There was no hurry to reach the ridge; the small details were the reason for coming.

> Notice what changes when you slow down: the shape of a shadow, a bird crossing the water, the first light in a distant window.

The guide should feel like an invitation, not a checklist. Keep the directions clear and leave the reader enough space to discover something of their own.

## Notes for the next draft

## Returning home

By evening the water had turned the color of slate. We took the lower path back, carrying a handful of observations and a much better opening sentence.
