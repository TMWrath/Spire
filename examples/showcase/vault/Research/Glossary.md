# A working glossary

## Wayfinding

The signs, landmarks, and decisions that help a person understand a route.

## Field note

A short observation recorded close to the moment of discovery.

## Editorial pass

A focused review of one aspect of a draft, such as clarity, structure, or tone.

Use these terms while reviewing [[Projects/Lantern Trail/Editorial checklist]].
